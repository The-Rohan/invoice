The user corrected the initial readings to Matmal, Amir Shehzad Matmal, **ملی**, and **چڑا**. The smaller supplier/printer text on the sign is not a business contact and is not copied into invoice headers. No street address or manager phone number was supplied, so those fields remain empty instead of retaining another business's details.

The app header and dashboard show the business and contacts. New invoices use these details; selected fish names appear in the line descriptions and species-weight summary. Every row's rate is still negotiated independently. Additional/custom fish names can be typed normally and are suggested after saving.

Existing installations with the completely untouched original sample business settings receive the new defaults on startup. Previously saved invoice headers are preserved, including older records missing a business snapshot. Customized business settings are not overwritten. In Business settings, **Use details from the shop sign** fills the fields for review; **Save settings** applies them to new bills.

The original 17-row invoice example retains its original fish descriptions and exact arithmetic as a reference. The new fish suggestions are independent of that fixture. The existing README screenshots/PDF were captured before this branding update and need regeneration when browser execution is available.

Validation for this update: eight local calculation, invoice-rendering and business-migration tests passed. Browser assertions were updated but have not been rerun.
